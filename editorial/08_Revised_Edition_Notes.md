## 1. What this version is

You asked what the book would look like if every structural change in `02_Structure_and_Flow_Plan.md` were actually made, the way an editor would do it if they had to make the book ready for publication.

This is that version. It is a demonstration, not a replacement.

- **It is still your book.** It keeps your plot, your cast, your set pieces, your ending and, wherever a scene survives, your sentences. Most of the text is your edited prose carried across and trimmed.
- **Some of it is not yours.** About 13,000 words are new scenes and bridges that my team wrote in your voice, so the structural plan could be carried out. Section 4 lists all of them. Each one is a proposal. The strongest use of this version is to read it, decide which changes you want, and then write those scenes again yourself. An agent is buying *your* voice.
- **It has no tracked changes.** A structural revision moves, merges and rewrites whole chapters, so a redline against the original would be unreadable. Use Section 3 (the chapter map) to compare it with the original or with the line-edited version.
- **The other two versions are unchanged.** The tracked and clean line edits still stand on their own. This third version builds on the clean line edit, with every line-edit change accepted.

## 2. The shape of the new book

**Title:** *The Tell*. The word has three meanings in the book, and the text never explains them:

1. **A poker tell.** Bradley tells her "Every player at that table has a tell", and she finds none on Benedikt.
2. **Benedikt's tell.** His face breaks when he sees the photograph of her father on the ship, and it is the first crack in the famous poker face.
3. **Telling.** Her father never told his captors where the evidence was for four years, and Benedikt finally tells her everything.

The running header now reads *The Tell*. It used to say *Until I Find You*, an older title still in your file's header.

**Length:** about 81,000 words in 60 chapters, down from 85 chapters. The line-edited version is 78,500 words and your original is 82,100.

| Part | Chapters | Words | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|

### The main structural changes

1. **Chronological order.** Every dateline now runs forward, apart from two deliberate rewinds (Chapters 3 and 14), and each rewind is flagged in its opening sentence.
2. **Part Two was cut and ended on the heist briefing.** The job-application documents were reduced to a line each. A new closing chapter (13) shows the scene the whole book rests on: Bradley's briefing, the honey trap as *her* idea, and "I need a driver in Switzerland".
3. **Retold scenes were removed.** Each event is now told once, from the viewpoint that learns the most from it. This applies to her escape, the Zurich arrival, the flight, the Lisbon arrival, the jet landing and the shooting.
4. **The Adriana sequence** is now four chapters (22–26) instead of eight, with a Part break at the end rather than in the middle of a gunpoint scene.
5. **The two long monologues were halved.** Her life story in Tallinn (33) now shows Benedikt's reactions. Maxwell's confession (35) gets a reason and an admission: "I knew about your father when I put you through that year. I used it. You were eighteen."
6. **The ship Part was trimmed.** It lost four rounds of Benedikt reproaching himself and three repeated arguments. It gained one concrete document (the insurance schedule) and one scene: the Cham statue in the broken crate at Gdynia.
7. **The ending was rebuilt around the rescue.** In your draft the rescue happens off the page. Now it is on the page:
   - Yuri tells the middle of her father's story (51).
   - Her reasoning about forgiving Benedikt is shown (53).
   - Benedikt confesses his part in the Range Rover in 2010, on the night in Russia (57).
   - The villains set the trap (58).
   - The reunion takes place, and her father tells the beginning in his own voice (59).
   - The evidence under her windowsill is recovered, and her mother is heard on the phone (60).
   - Evan Slater is arrested, reported by text.
   - The last image is the misted rear window, wiped with her sleeve, and the empty bend in the road.

   There is no epilogue and no sequel tease.
8. **Every loose end is closed.** This covers the dead officer, the wounded henchman, the doctor, Adriana, Stephen's question about Maxwell, Maxwell's authority, the tail Kozlov ordered, and what her father carried.

### Invented or settled facts

- **What Daniel carried.** A gilded Cham temple statuette, about a thousand years old, from the Mỹ Sơn temples in Vietnam. It was waybilled as "garden statuary, reproduction" and handed to him on Evan Slater's instruction on 2 February 2010.

- **Why he was kept alive.** His bluff about the pictures going to the police. Later he was also leverage over Evan.
- **Her age and name.** She is 16, 18 and 20. Her mother is Isabel, née Sánchez, from Vigo, and "Sanchez" is her mother's maiden name. Only her father calls her "San".
- **Benedikt Voss.** He is German-born, from Flensburg, raised in Denmark, and was a junior hire in the Range Rover in 2010. In October 2013, at a café in Vitebsk, he said "get rid of him".

## 5. How it was made

1. **A story bible.** One document fixed the true chronology, the cast, every scene of the new book, every plant and payoff, and the rules of the world: time zones, sunrise times, borders and law. Every writer worked from it.
2. **Twelve writer-editors**, each taking about five chapters. They carried your edited text across by default, cut by deletion, and wrote new material only where the plan required it. They worked from the same style studies as the line edit.
3. **Three whole-book readers** read the revised draft for timeline, character consistency and developmental problems. A set of editor-in-chief rulings settled the conflicts between their reports, for example the flight times, the sedative and the safety catch on the Makarov.
4. **Six fixers**, one per Part, applied those rulings. Six proofreaders then cold-read the book for grammar, British usage, hyphenation and repetition.
5. **Mechanical checks** ran on every chapter. They banned em dashes, new semicolons, colons in narration, a list of AI-typical words and gestures, American spellings, and conflicts with the bible. Repeated phrases were given book-wide limits, for example "What took you so long?" once and the lightning image once.
6. **Assembly.** The Word file uses your original manuscript as its template, so fonts, margins and page setup are yours. It passes schema validation.

## 7. Caveats

- **The new scenes are drafts in your voice. They are not your voice.** They passed the checks, but a reader who knows your writing well will feel the joins in places. Rewrite them.
- **Facts.** The 2014 details (flight times, the Belarus–Russia border regime, AIS tracking, NCA procedure) were checked against public information but not against primary sources. Check them before you submit.
- **No visual render.** As before, the Word file was checked structurally but not opened in Word here. Chapters start on a new page, datelines are bold italic, and scene breaks are a centred asterisk.
- **Formatting is simpler than your original.** It uses plain body paragraphs, with no superscript "th" in datelines.
- **Title.** *The Tell* is my first choice of the three in `04_Title_Options.md`. The other two work equally well with this structure.
