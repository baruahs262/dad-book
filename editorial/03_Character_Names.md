# Character Names: Reassessment

You asked me to reassess the character names and make changes. My approach was conservative. Names are part of your vision, so I changed one only when there was a clear reason:

- two names in the same stretch of the book that readers could confuse;
- a name wrong for the character's nationality;
- an unintended meaning, brand or famous echo;
- a typo or variant spelling.

**All the leads keep their names:** Sanchez, Benedikt, Daniel, Stephen and Maxwell.

**How to accept or reject.** Every name change in the tracked manuscript is recorded under the reviewer name **"Editor (Names)"**. In Word, go to *Review → Show Markup → Specific People* and show only "Editor (Names)". You can then accept or reject them all at once, or one by one. If you reject a change here, reject it everywhere it appears so the name stays consistent.

The only exceptions are a few name misspellings (Curz → Cruz, Dave → David and similar) that were fixed inside the ordinary line edit where the sentence was being corrected anyway.

## A note on "Sanchez"

To settle "Sanchez" and "Roberti", add one sentence at the first natural backstory point. Good places are chapter 11 (her arrival at Aunt Nina's) or her story to Benedikt in chapter 50. A sentence in your register might read:

> *Sanchez was her mother's maiden name, given to her in the old Scottish way; her father, whose grandfather had come to Ayrshire from Italy, used to joke that she had inherited the whole of the Mediterranean in two words.*

This one line explains a Scottish girl with a Spanish first name and an Italian surname. It also explains why Portuguese was her "third language" (ch 24).
