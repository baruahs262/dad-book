# The Last Scintilla: Edited Manuscript Package

**Start here.**

1. **Read `editorial/01_Editorial_Letter.md`.** It gives the verdict, what works, what to fix, flow, names, title options and next steps.
2. **Open `The-Last-Scintilla-EDITED-tracked.docx` in Microsoft Word.**
   - Every edit is a tracked change (red lines). Accept or reject each one.
   - The margin comments explain the larger changes and ask questions.
   - Changes are grouped under four reviewer names, so you can handle each kind in one go: **Editor** (line edit), **Editor (Names)**, **Editor (Spelling)** and **Editor (Typography)**. Filter by name under *Review → Show Markup → Specific People*.
3. **Read the rest of the package:**
   - `The-Last-Scintilla-EDITED-clean.docx`: every change accepted, for reading straight through.
   - `Redline-Preview.html`: a read-only view of every change and comment that opens in any browser, including on a phone.
   - `book-edited.md`: the edited text as plain Markdown.
   - The `editorial/` folder: the structure plan (02), character names (03), title options (04), continuity and timeline (05), house style sheet (06), and notes on every decision made on your behalf (07).
   - The `editorial/appendix/` folder: the full reader reports and style studies the edit was built on.

## The third version: the structural revision

`The-Tell-REVISED-EDITION.docx` (and `book-revised.md`) is the book with every structural change in the plan actually made: 60 chapters in six Parts, events in order, retold scenes removed, the rescue and the ending on the page, and every loose end closed. It keeps your plot, cast, set pieces and sentences wherever a scene survives. About 13,000 words of new scenes were written in your voice to carry out the plan. They are proposals for you to rewrite, not your words. Read `editorial/08_Revised_Edition_Notes.md` first. It has the chapter map and a list of everything invented.

Your original files, `The-Last-Scintilla-Editing.docx` and `book.md`, are untouched.
